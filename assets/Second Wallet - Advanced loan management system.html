Summary

Second wallet offers top tier Financial loan services with competitive interest rates and repayment schedule. They set out to develop an in-house loan management system to manage loan requests, track repayments and disbursement.
As a UIUX designer with a wide range of expertise in Web3, Agro-tech, Pharma-tech, Aviation etc, embarking on a design for this complex problem was a challenge but it wasn't one I couldn't deliver.
This Case study narrates my experiences, Research and journey in designing one of my most complex products without sharing core proprietary information.

Project Goals

To design a user-friendly, efficient, and secure system that caters to both loan officers and management while ensuring regulatory compliance.
Simplify it enough for staffs to understand.
Make it robust enough to allow for future upgrades.

Problem

Managing Loan applications, disbursement and repayment is a tedious process without a system to simplify the process. A lot of paper work and bogus tracking process made the process take longer time that expected. These issues leads to
Delayed loan approvals and disbursements
Difficulty in tracking repayments and arrears
Lack of real-time financial insights
Inefficient staff performance tracking
Limited investor transparency
Second Wallet was designed to solve these inefficiencies by providing a robust, all-in-one solution that streamlines loan operations.

Research and Discovery

We conducted extensive research through:
User interviews with loan officers, Executives and financial analysts to ascertain their needs and pain point.
Competitive analysis of existing loan management systems
Pain point identification to prioritize critical features
Deep dive into The PRD ensuring that all of the clients need was considered and catered for.

Design Solutions

After research and consideration of all factors, We settled for these solution and features. (Not all mentioned.)
Loan and Borrower Management
Repayment and Arrears Tracking
Financial report and Portfolio health
Investor Log & Transparency, etc.

Design Process

Once research was concluded I got into designing the core system. My process includes.
Information Architect
Gathering Design inspiration
Wireframing
Design (The atomic design System)
Handover
Let’s dive into these processes:
Information Architecture and Site map: Before jumping into design, I mapped out user flows and structured the platform in a way that made sense for loan officers, borrowers, and investors. We ensured the main sections—borrower management, loan processing, repayments, financial reports, staff payroll, investor logs, and expense tracking—were easy to navigate, making sure everything flowed logically and efficiently.
Gathering Design inspiration: Before designing the wireframes, I explored successful financial platforms, analyzed best practices, and identified key UI/UX patterns that enhance usability and accessibility. Once Satisfied, I began wireframing.
Wireframing Though this process didn't take a lot of time or screens because of the urgency of delivery. I had to establish a structure that would work for the entire pages. This was achieved seamlessly once every feature and flow was considered.
Design: I have always found The Atomic Design System by Brad Frost as a comprehensive system that allows a structured design process. This consist of Atoms, Molecules, Organisms, Templates and Pages.
In the Atom Section, I established the base elements. These includes Typography, Color, Icons, Spacing, Corner Radius, Layout Grid, Spacers etc.
In the Molecule section, I formed basic elements such as Buttons, Text fields, Tooltips, Avatar, Alerts, Checkboxes, Toggles, Notification, Sort Icon, Badges, Dot and Tag count. These are small elements formed with the base elements already established in the atom section
Organisms: The organisms deals with the combination of Atoms and molecules. They are bigger component and allow for uniformity and easy upgrades across multiple screens. In here, I designed the Navigation and Pagination, Modals, Nav Bars and buttons, Top bar, Tables rows, Upload interface, Calendar, etc.
Templates: Templates housed the Combination of all the prior elements in a simpler form. This is where Wireframes and adequate page details comes in without attention drawn to aesthetics. the idea is to design functional pages. This process was challenging as I had to learn a lot about finance and structuring information for finance experts simplifying their process. We ended up designing over 150 pages.
Pages: In the Pages section, I designed the various pages with attention to details and aesthetic as this will be the final look.
