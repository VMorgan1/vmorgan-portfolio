FAQ

Questions clients usually ask before we start.

What kind of teams are you a great fit for?

I am a strong fit for founders, startups, agencies, and product teams that need someone who can understand the problem, shape the experience, design the interface, and work well with the people building it.

Can you work with an existing product or only new ideas?

Both. I can help shape a new product from scratch, improve an existing experience, redesign a dashboard, make onboarding clearer, or prepare a product for the next stage of growth.

What do you need from me before starting?

A short brief, the product goal, target audience, timeline, and any existing links, screenshots, brand assets, or references. If the idea is still rough, we can clarify it together during the first call.

Do you also prepare designs for developers?

Yes. I can include organized files, responsive states, interaction notes, reusable patterns, and build guidance so the implementation team has less guesswork.

How soon can we begin?

That depends on the scope and current availability, but the fastest next step is to book a call or send a short brief. From there, I can confirm timeline, deliverables, and the best way to approach the work.
